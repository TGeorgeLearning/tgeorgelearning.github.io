CHIME: A Constrained Harmonic Inductive Bias for Melody Extraction

This website serves as the demo page for the CHIME model. Below are a few demonstrations, where we display an excerpt of a song, along with the synthesized sinusoid audio representing CHIME's predicted melody contours. For comparison, we also synthesize and present the predicted melody contours of competing methods. This page consists of three short song demos, along with an entire song demo presented at the bottom of this page. The songs selected are copyright-free, or are allowed for public use by the creators of the song.

Audio comparisons

Each column of songs contains the original and synthesized sinusoidal audios from competing models for a given song. Some audio samples by competing models contain a large amount of audible jitter, or sounds that disturb what is otherwise a smooth melody contour. This is due to how these models can rapidly swap between voiced and unvoiced predictions, resulting in temporally incoherent predictions.
